What are you trying to do?

How do I take a panel and its legend into my slides?

Select the panel to save a PNG from the PDF. Adjust the crop or mark a shared legend, axis or scale bar. Queue several panels and export a PowerPoint with your speaking notes and source pages.

What does this point or axis tell me?

In panel 8A, click the x-axis to see how ρ changes attraction in t-SNE. Select Fashion MNIST in the legend or click its peak to read the setting where the two layouts agree most closely.

Which groups are compared, and what happened first?

The reading guide brings identifiable study designs and event order beside the figure. Look for the intervention, comparison group and measurement, then ask what the endpoint tests. Where a checked pathology guide is available, it links tissue features to the corresponding quantitative score.

Open the reader ↗
Can I translate a term, then ask a more specific question?

Select text to translate it or ask about it. When the paper defines an abbreviation, the reader can show its expansion and page. Questions carry the selected text and figure context so you can check an answer against the caption or passage.

Watch a question in context ↗
How do I turn individual figures into a talk?

The map links the research focus to figures and panels. Follow a branch back to its evidence. You can also load supplementary files and references alongside the main paper when you want to check a related figure or method.

Explore the paper map ↗
How do I pick up where I left off?

Write a private note beside the figure and save the reading position to your private map. Reopen the paper in the same browser to return to the source, notes and figure you saved.

Open the reader ↗
Can I look up background papers or use a different AI service?

The browser offers a source guide, Crossref lookup and a handoff to a web AI chat of your choice. The portable Windows edition adds PubMed lookup and in-reader connections to DeepSeek, Qwen, an installed Ollama model or Codex CLI. Python is included in the download.
